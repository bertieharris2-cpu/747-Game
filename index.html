Emergency Landing

A Boeing 747 maths rescue

Boeing 747. You're in the cockpit. Dodge the birds, put out the engine fire, save the passengers falling out, and land the plane. You'll need maths to do it. Come on down to the 747!

How to play
  • Each emergency is solved with maths. Right answers keep the plane flying and passengers on board.
  • Tap an answer, or press 1–4. For birds you can also use the ← ↑ → arrow keys.
  • For typed answers, use the keypad or your keyboard, then Enter.
  • Wrong? No problem – you'll see the right answer and a quick tip.
  • Press P or the ⏸ button to pause.
  • Land with as many passengers as you can. Don't hit the control tower!
